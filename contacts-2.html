Contacts

Fennec Trail is a one-man team from Turin, Italy.
I am Federico Barbero, software developer, designer and gamer. I love gaming in all its forms, video games, role-playing games, boardgames, sports, playing cards…
Over the years I have cultivated this passion for games and converted it into a job opportunity by starting my career as an indie developer in 2011. From 2013 to 2017 I worked as a freelancer developer on several projects that are available on Steam, Switch, PS4.

Few years ago I started working as a Solution Architect with the aim to add new skills to my portfolio, such as Artificial Intelligence, IT/Software Architecture, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, Project Management…
At the same time, I plan to consolidate the coding skills I have acquired during the first years of my career.
As a true gamer I love challenges and in 2019 I decided to start a solo project to put my experience to the test. It all started with a simple prototype of a first-person runner that later turned into 1984 Rewired, my first fully self-produced game.
The challenge now continues with a new project and several more prototypes in the pipeline!
