Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17/07/2025

Welcome to Fennec Trail. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile applications (“apps”).

1. Data Collection

Our apps do not collect any personal information from users. We do not gather names, email addresses, locations, or any other personally identifiable data.

2. Advertisements

Our apps may display advertisements served by third-party services such as Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your age:

  • If you confirm you are 18 years or older, you may see personalized ads based on your preferences.

  • If you do not confirm you are 18 or older, the apps will show only child-appropriate, non-personalized ads, in compliance with laws protecting children's privacy, including COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K (European Union regulations on children’s data).

Google AdMob is certified to serve ads compliant with relevant privacy regulations for children.

3. Age Verification

To comply with privacy regulations, our apps ask users to confirm if they are 18 years or older upon first use. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted or shared with any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services like Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Please review their privacy policies for more details:

  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We encourage you to check this page periodically for updates.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: info@fennectrail.com
